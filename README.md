# Ciao by WorksBuddy: AI Voice Agent for Inbound and Outbound Calls

Ciao is the AI voice agent in the [WorksBuddy](https://worksbuddy.ai/) suite. It answers inbound calls, makes outbound calls, holds natural conversations, and turns every call into a clear result your team can act on.

**Website:** https://worksbuddy.ai/ciao

**Pricing:** https://worksbuddy.ai/ciao/pricing

## Why it exists

A buyer who calls after hours, or while your team is busy, rarely leaves a voicemail. They call the next business on the list. Ciao is built to pick up, understand what the caller needs, and move toward a useful next step, such as an answer, a booked appointment, or a transfer to a person.

## What Ciao does

### Inbound calls
- Answers calls at any hour, including after hours and weekends
- Answers questions from your own documents (PDFs, Word files, or plain text)
- Checks availability and books appointments during the call
- Transfers calls to your team based on rules you define

### Outbound calls
- Calls new leads within minutes and asks your qualifying questions
- Passes interested buyers to your sales team
- Sends polite payment reminder calls before and after due dates
- Re-calls older inquiries to find who is ready now

### Built for real conversations
- **Interruption handling.** Callers can cut in, correct themselves, or change the topic.
- **Real-time responses.** No long pauses while the agent "thinks".
- **Multilingual support.** Detects the caller's language and can follow switches between Hindi and English mid-sentence.
- **Grounded answers.** Replies come from the content you upload, not guesses.

### Clear outcomes
Every call produces something your team can use:
- Full transcript and a short recap
- A result such as qualified, booked, transferred, or closed
- Outcome synced to your workflow or lead record

## Who it's for

- **Founders:** take calls while you build
- **Front desks and bookings:** answer the phone and log the booking
- **Sales and inside sales:** pick up missed calls, qualify, and book meetings
- **Support teams:** reduce hold times
- **Product and engineering:** place calls from your own product and get transcripts and outcomes back
- **Agencies and multi-brand teams:** separate numbers, voices, and content per brand

## How it works

1. **Choose the job.** Start with lead qualification, inquiry handling, payment reminders, or your own workflow.
2. **Teach it your business.** Upload PDFs, Word files, or plain text.
3. **Test it.** Try the full agent in your browser, tune the voice, and set transfer rules.
4. **Connect and go live.** Connect your number, publish, and save each call result to your lead record.

## Responsible use

- Tell callers they are speaking with an AI.
- Call recording is optional and configurable per agent. Follow the consent and disclosure rules that apply in your region.
- Test your agent yourself before real customers do: interrupt it, switch languages, and ask unexpected questions.
